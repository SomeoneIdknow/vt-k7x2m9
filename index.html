Weekly check-in

Sunday morning is the easiest.

How to do it
  • Weight: right after waking, before eating or drinking, after peeing if you can. Same scale, same spot, light clothes. Skipping the bathroom is fine, just be consistent.
  • Waist: tape at belly button height, level all round. Breathe out normally, do not suck in, snug but not tight. Measure twice, use the average.
  • Photos: front, side, back, every 2 weeks.
